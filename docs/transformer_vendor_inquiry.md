# Draft technical inquiry - not sent

Subject: LL1679/PP for a high-source-impedance differential audio DAC

We are evaluating a simulation-only differential resistor/switch DAC driving an
8 ohm load through an output transformer. Our working candidate is LL1679/PP
with the standard silicon-iron core, full primary and secondary alternative B.
The source has approximately 10.55 kohm differential Thevenin resistance.
Nominal primary DC is zero. Our target is 5 W into 8 ohm over 20 Hz-20 kHz;
10 W is a stretch target, not established performance. All executed switched
simulations are at 5 V; 600-700 V is an analytical system-design range only.

Could you provide or confirm:

- Exact ordering designation, price and lead time for one PP/standard-core unit.
- Loaded impedance for alternative B into 8 ohm, including winding losses.
- Primary inductance at 20 Hz, 100 Hz and 1 kHz versus voltage, and its tolerance.
- Permissible residual DC imbalance and its effect on inductance and distortion.
- Open/short-circuit impedance sweeps or equivalent-circuit/SPICE data, including
  effective capacitance, resonances and frequency-dependent core loss.
- Suitable 20 Hz primary signal voltage for 5 W and 10 W secondary output, with
  the measurement conditions and distortion criteria stated.
- Suitability for our source impedance and pulsed excitation at 192 kS/s.
- Applicable continuous working-voltage/insulation limits for this use, separate
  from dielectric test voltage, and any restrictions on a floating primary.

We would also appreciate advice on whether LL1620/PP would provide a meaningful
advantage for these conditions. We are not assuming the catalog power or
bandwidth ratings apply unchanged to our drive circuit.
